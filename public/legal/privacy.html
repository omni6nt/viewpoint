Privacy Notice

Viewpoint is a free, informational dashboard for understanding $TRUST bonding on the Intuition network. This notice explains what information is processed when you use it.

What Viewpoint is

Viewpoint reads public, already-on-chain data from the Intuition network (via its public RPC), and from public ENS and TNS naming services. It does not require you to connect a wallet, sign a transaction, or create an account.

What information is processed

  • Wallet addresses or names you type in are sent only to the relevant public blockchain RPC (Intuition, Ethereum for ENS, or TNS) to look up publicly available on-chain data. This is the same as looking up an address on any public block explorer.
  • Recently checked wallets are saved in your browser's local storage so they appear as suggestions next time. This data stays on your device. It is never sent to or stored on any Viewpoint server, because Viewpoint has no server or database.

What Viewpoint does not do

  • No user accounts or logins.
  • No tracking scripts, or advertising cookies.
  • No selling, sharing, or storing of your personal information. There is nothing collected to sell or share.

Third parties

Looking up data involves public RPC providers (Intuition, Ethereum, TNS). These are independent services not operated by Viewpoint; their own privacy practices apply to the network requests they receive.

Changes

This notice may be updated as Viewpoint's features change. Check back here for the current version.
