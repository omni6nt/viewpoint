Local storage notice

Viewpoint saves the wallet addresses, ENS names, or TNS names you've recently checked directly in your browser's local storage, so they appear as quick suggestions the next time you visit.

This information stays entirely on your own device. It is never transmitted to, or stored on, any Viewpoint server, because Viewpoint doesn't have one. Clearing your browser's site data for this page will remove it.

Viewpoint does not use cookies, tracking pixels, or third-party advertising scripts.
